Code Font Bench

Thin, wide monospace faces next to Courier New, rendered at the exact pixel size your editor uses. Keep browser zoom at 100% so 12 here equals "editor.fontSize": 12. Courier New comes from your own system; everything else is embedded.

Courier New’s valueStem weight is the vertical stroke width relative to Courier New. x-height ÷ width is how tall lowercase letters stand in their cell; lower reads wider and airier.
